Hermina Garic

Lead Policy Researcher

Hermina Garic works in the non-profit sector to end domestic violence and sexual violence within Herkimer and Oneida counties of New York.

She holds a Bachelor's degree in Sociology/Anthropology, Political Science, and International Studies from Utica University. Her research focuses on the international economic development of countries impacted by the intersections of colonialism and imperialism.
